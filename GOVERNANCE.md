# Governance

- **Maintainers** merge changes and cut releases.
- **Technical steering group** (at least one independent coastal engineer, one fish-welfare scientist, one environmental lawyer, and one Indigenous fisheries representative) reviews any change to thresholds, flags or model language before release.
- **Releases** are tagged, archived on Zenodo with a DOI, and listed in `CHANGELOG.md`. Notebooks and the web app state the version they use.
- **Conflicts of interest**: steering-group members declare funding from data-centre operators, utilities or aquaculture companies.
- **Model validation policy**: a module moves from "Needs benchmark" to "Verified" only after the comparison is published in `docs/`.
- **Disclaimer**: outputs are screening results for scoping and public understanding. They are not legal advice and not a substitute for a regulatory mixing-zone study, a §316 demonstration, a Fisheries Act authorisation or species-expert review.
