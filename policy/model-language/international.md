# International and United Kingdom

## A. BBNJ Agreement, COP-1 (11 to 22 January 2027)

The Agreement entered into force on 17 January 2026. Its environmental impact assessment provisions apply to activities in areas beyond national jurisdiction.

<!-- clause: bbnj-cop1 -->
We invite the Scientific and Technical Body to develop guidance on environmental impact assessment for continuous heat-rejecting infrastructure, including floating and submerged data centres, covering thermal discharge, cooling-water intake, underwater noise, electromagnetic fields from power cables, and chemical releases, and to encourage Parties to publish monitoring data through the Clearing-House Mechanism.
<!-- /clause -->

## B. ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 39: new work item

The ISO/IEC 30134 series defines PUE, REF, ERF, CER, CUE and WUE. None measures heat discharged to water or intake effects.

<!-- clause: iso-proposal -->
Proposed technical report: "Data centres: key performance indicators for thermal discharge to surface water and cooling-water intake", defining heat-to-water intensity (MWh discharged to surface water per MWh of IT energy), maximum discharge temperature rise, design intake intensity (m³ per MWh of IT energy) and through-screen velocity, with reporting guidance for organisms impinged and entrained.
<!-- /clause -->

## C. UK Animal Sentience Committee

The Animal Welfare (Sentience) Act 2022 recognises vertebrates, cephalopod molluscs and decapod crustaceans as sentient and created the Committee to report on whether government policy has regard to their welfare. Data centres were designated Critical National Infrastructure in September 2024.

<!-- clause: uk-asc -->
We ask the Committee to consider whether policy on the siting, cooling and heat reuse of data centres designated as Critical National Infrastructure has regard to the welfare of fishes and decapod crustaceans affected by cooling-water intakes and thermal discharges, and by aquaculture supplied with data-centre heat.
<!-- /clause -->
