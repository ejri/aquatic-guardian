# European Union: consultation response and model amendments

Status, 29 September 2026:
- The Commission adopted a Delegated Regulation establishing an EU sustainability rating scheme for data centres of 500 kW and above on 21 September 2026, built on reporting under Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1364. It is in the two-month scrutiny period; first labels are expected in 2027 and a review is due by 31 December 2028.
- A consultation on binding minimum performance standards for data centres closes on **14 December 2026**; a legislative proposal is expected in spring 2027.
- The Cloud and AI Development Act (COM(2026) 502, 3 June 2026) is a Commission proposal in the ordinary legislative procedure. It would create acceleration zones with permitting within 12 months, including authorisations for water abstraction, wastewater discharge and heat.

## A. Draft response to the minimum performance standards consultation

<!-- clause: eu-mps-response -->
We support binding minimum performance standards for data centres and ask the Commission to include the effect of cooling on surface waters, which current reporting under Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1364 does not capture.

Water use effectiveness measures consumptive freshwater use. A data centre cooled with seawater or river water, or sited under water, can report near-zero water use while discharging most of its electricity demand as heat to a water body and drawing large volumes of water, with the organisms in it, through its intakes. Heat is recognised as a form of energy capable of causing marine pollution under UNCLOS, and water temperature is a supporting element of ecological status under the Water Framework Directive.

We propose three additions for data centres that withdraw or discharge surface water or seawater: (1) a reporting indicator for heat discharged to surface water (MWh per year) and the maximum discharge temperature rise; (2) a reporting indicator for design intake flow and through-screen velocity, with impingement and entrainment reported by taxon and life stage; (3) a minimum standard that new data centres of 1 MW or more use closed-loop or air-rejected cooling unless the operator demonstrates, in the environmental assessment, that once-through cooling causes less harm at that site.

We also ask that heat supplied to aquaculture count toward waste-heat reuse only where farmed animals are kept to a recognised welfare standard, consistent with Article 13 TFEU.

A machine-readable schema for these indicators, with a crosswalk to Delegated Regulation 2024/1364, is published under an open licence at [repository link].

## B. Amendments to the Cloud and AI Development Act

### B1. Acceleration zones

<!-- clause: eu-cada-zones -->
In the article on the designation of acceleration zones, add: "Before designating an acceleration zone, Member States shall identify water bodies within or adjacent to the zone that could receive cooling-water discharges or be subject to cooling-water abstraction, and shall assess whether data centres using such waters could affect the status of those water bodies under Directive 2000/60/EC or the integrity of sites under Directive 92/43/EEC. Zones shall not include areas where such effects cannot be excluded without an appropriate assessment."
<!-- /clause -->

### B2. Simplified permitting

<!-- clause: eu-cada-resource -->
In the provision on resource-efficiency requirements for simplified permitting, add: "Simplified permitting shall not be available for data centres that use once-through cooling with surface water or seawater unless the operator has demonstrated compliance with the thermal and intake indicators established under Directive (EU) 2023/1791."
<!-- /clause -->

## C. For the 2028 rating-scheme review

Add the heat-to-surface-water and intake indicators from section A to the label, so that a seawater-cooled data centre cannot achieve a top water rating solely because its freshwater consumption is low.

## D. Member states with heat-reuse mandates

Germany's Energy Efficiency Act (EnEfG) requires new data centres operating from 1 July 2026 to reach PUE 1.2 or lower and an energy reuse factor of at least 10 %, rising to 15 % from 1 July 2027 and 20 % from 1 July 2028.

### D1. Energy reuse and animal welfare

<!-- clause: eu-enefg -->
Heat supplied to the rearing of animals shall be counted toward the energy reuse factor only if the recipient facility complies with [a recognised welfare standard for each species], and the operator reports the species, stocking density and mortality at the recipient facility.
<!-- /clause -->
