## A. BC Hydro call for power: scoring criterion

<!-- clause: bc-scoring -->
Aquatic and marine impact (weight: [x] %). Applicants shall submit an Aquatic Disclosure Record at design values. Scores are awarded for: (a) closed-loop or air-rejected cooling, or once-through cooling with a design intake below 0.088 m³/s; (b) heat recovered for beneficial use before discharge, as an energy reuse factor; (c) siting outside critical habitat under the Species at Risk Act, marine protected areas and eelgrass and kelp beds; (d) a monitoring and data-sharing plan co-developed with the First Nations whose territories include the receiving waters. An application proposing once-through cooling into waters within the territory of a First Nation shall include evidence of engagement with that Nation.
<!-- /clause -->

## B. Fisheries and Oceans Canada guidance

<!-- clause: dfo-guidance -->
Cooling water discharged from a data centre at a temperature that would, if added to the receiving water, degrade or alter its quality so as to be deleterious to fish or fish habitat is a deleterious substance within the meaning of subsection 34(1) of the Fisheries Act. Operators should obtain a thermal assessment at critical design conditions before discharge, and intakes should be designed to avoid impingement and entrainment consistent with DFO guidance on intake screening and a Fisheries Act authorisation where section 35 applies.
<!-- /clause -->

## C. ISED contribution agreements

<!-- clause: ised-condition -->
The recipient shall report an Aquatic Disclosure Record annually for each funded facility that withdraws or discharges surface water or seawater. Heat reused for aquaculture shall be supplied only to facilities that keep animals to [a recognised welfare standard].
